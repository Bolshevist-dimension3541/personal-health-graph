## 📋 What is this system

Personal-health-graph stores your medical records in one place. It creates a structured file of your health data. You control this file. You choose which AI tools see your data. It protects your privacy while helping AI understand your unique health needs.

## 🚀 Getting started

Open the application from your Start Menu after installation. The first time you launch the program, the system builds your local database. This process takes a few seconds. You will see a dashboard with empty fields. You fill these fields with your health information.

## ✏️ Adding your data

The system uses a simple form structure. You type your information into the boxes. You can log medical conditions, lab results, and medication history. The application saves these entries as a graph. A graph links your data points. For example, it connects a specific medication you take to a health condition you track. This structure helps AI tools read your history later.

## 🛡️ Protecting your privacy

This application works locally. Your data stays on your computer. It does not send your health records to the cloud unless you choose to share them. You keep full control over your information. If you want to use an AI tool, you export your graph file and provide it to the AI. You decide which details appear in that file.

## 💡 Using your health graph

You export your graph as a JSON file. Most modern AI tools accept this format. When you talk to an AI about your health, upload your graph file first. The AI receives your full medical context. It offers better advice because it knows your specific health history.

## 📝 Frequently asked questions

**Does this app connect to my doctor?**
No. This app holds your records manually. You enter the data yourself.

**Can I delete my data?**
Yes. You delete the folder where the application saves your graph records to erase everything.

**Does it track my location?**
No. The application has no internet connection features. It functions entirely offline.

## 🔍 How the data structure works

The system organizes your health data into nodes and edges. A node represents a data point, like a blood pressure reading. An edge represents the relationship, like the date of that reading. This method mirrors how your body systems work together. It provides a clearer picture than a simple list or spreadsheet.

## 🗄️ Organizing your files

Keep your graph file in a folder you can find easily. Back up this file to an external hard drive or a secure USB stick. If you buy a new computer, copy the file to your new machine. Install the application on the new machine and load your file. Your health history moves with you.

## 📈 Improving your experience
